Why this direction

Live Foundry leans frosty glass + blue→indigo glossy CTAs. That reads “SaaS default.” This mock keeps your locked grounds (#0e1111 / #F2F0EF) but switches finish to matte instrument: solid CTAs, quiet borders, tonal depth, no glow bloom, no rainbow gradients. Dark CTA is chalk (same off-white family) so both themes share one identity.

Leaving — frosty gloss
